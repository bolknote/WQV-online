Original watch manuals
----------------------

The reference documents in dist/manuals/ are converted from the CASIO module
2220 and 2411 user manuals. English and Japanese sources are published by
CASIO COMPUTER CO., LTD.; Russian editions are provided by i-Watch.Ru.
The original manuals, illustrations and translations retain their original
copyright and are not covered by this application's MIT license.
Source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in dist/manuals/manifest.json.

Browser C driver toolchain
-------------------------

dist/driver-runtime/compiler/ contains the WCC browser compiler, headers and
C libraries from tyfkda/xcc (MIT, copyright 2024 tyfkda). Its full license is
compiler/LICENSE-MIT. The distributed demo archive is identified in
compiler/provenance.json; it is not claimed to be built from the separately
recorded reference source commit.

dist/driver-runtime/vendor/wasi/package/ contains browser_wasi_shim 0.4.2
by bjorn3, under MIT OR Apache-2.0. Both license texts and package metadata
are included next to its JavaScript files.

The original Linux driver files and headers under
dist/driver-runtime/corpus/sources/ retain their source notices and licenses.
They are not licensed under the application's MIT license. Linux source
versions, immutable URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in corpus/sources.json.
The GPL v2 text is supplied in corpus/COPYING. These sources are provided in
full, without changes. The WQV compatibility headers and entry wrappers are
separate files, compiled with the selected source in the browser.
